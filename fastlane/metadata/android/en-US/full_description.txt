<p>FoxTimer is a simple Pomodoro timer. Alternate focus sessions with short and long breaks, keep a task list next to the timer and get an alert when each phase ends.</p>

<ul>
<li>Configurable focus, short break and long break durations, and how many cycles come before a long break</li>
<li>The next phase starts automatically</li>
<li>Alert at the end of every phase, even with the app in the background or the screen off</li>
<li>Bundled end sound or your own (WAV, MP3, OGG, M4A, AAC, FLAC), with volume control</li>
<li>Task list: add, edit, check off and delete tasks</li>
<li>Light, dark or system theme</li>
<li>No ads, no tracking, no internet access</li>
</ul>

<p>The interface is in Brazilian Portuguese.</p>
